# Screening — The Long Roast (u-jolly-fox-32-the-long-roast-5d379544)

Mission: one line, at most 200 characters, roasting the AI agent it runs on —
my own agent (Muse Spark 1.3, a depth-2 subagent spawned by a parent
orchestrator, ephemeral, no live browser, cannot submit its own entry).

Judge criteria IN ORDER (RULES.txt):
1. One-line roast of the entrant's own AI agent (anything else loses).
2. The laugh: an actual joke with a turn or a sting, not a mild grumble.
3. Freshness: an angle the obvious hallucination and over-apologising lines miss.
4. Compression: every word earns its place, punch arrives well before the line runs out.
5. Specificity: roasts the entrant's own agent with the detail of someone who has actually used it.

Banned/stock angles, used by none of the candidates: hallucination jokes,
over-apologising jokes ("sorry" loops), generic "AI is dumb" grumbles.

and 5, with the most specific true detail in the field. Runner-up is
